**Preprint** — [arXiv:2505.05488](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05488).

A survey of event cameras for imaging. It starts from a physical model of event sensors and their characteristics, reviews how events are used in image and video enhancement, covers tasks that use events to capture richer light information (such as light-field estimation and multi-view generation), and closes with open challenges.
