**Accepted** — ACM SIGKDD 2026, Datasets &amp; Benchmarks Track. († co-first author, * corresponding author.)

SafeBuild-Bench is a construction-site safety benchmark built with graph-enhanced data mining, designed to be temporal-robust: it exposes how standard i.i.d. evaluation overstates the real-deployment reliability of vision-language models. The benchmark anchors my broader agenda on trustworthy, process-level verification of multimodal systems.
