**Under review** — ACL 2027. (* corresponding author.)

This work looks inside the verification layer of multimodal reasoning: it diagnoses a hidden *position-prior* confounder that inflates Step-AUC and other multimodal process-reward metrics, and characterizes the capability trade-offs these aggregate scores obscure.
