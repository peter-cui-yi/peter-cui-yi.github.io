**Under review** — ICLR 2027.

A vision–language model that answers an image question correctly may follow a regional edit that changes the answer-relevant facts, or it may keep its original answer. This paper asks whether measurements on the original image can rank these two responses in multiple-choice VQA, without observing the edited image.

**GLANCE** is a three-view score fixed before evaluation. It averages the support for the original answer on the full image and on an enlarged crop of the region, then subtracts the support that remains after the region is masked. Edited images supply the labels and decide which cases are admitted, but they never enter the score.

On six model-specific Visual7W cohorts, GLANCE ranks edit-following above preservation at **AUROC 0.707–0.832** and exceeds the strongest tested attention and geometry readouts in five of them. In matched diagnostic probes, adding masked-view support to the visible views raises AUROC by 0.124–0.243. The score ranks response tendencies under the tested edits; among changed answers, it does not consistently separate the target answer from a third option.
